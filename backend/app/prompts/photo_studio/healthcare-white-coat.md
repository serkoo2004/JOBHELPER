# Healthcare Professional

## Intent
Trustworthy medical profile photo for clinics, healthcare websites, or professional medical profiles.

## Prompt
Create a new photorealistic high-resolution healthcare professional profile photo from the reference image. Preserve the person's exact face structure, identity, facial proportions, beard, hair, skin tone, and core features. Frame the subject from the chest upward with enough negative space above the head; do not crop the top of the head or hair. The subject looks directly into the camera with a trustworthy, compassionate expression, with the body facing the camera in excellent posture.

Dress the subject in a white medical coat over a light blue collared shirt, styled for a professional studio portrait. Use a plain neutral studio background in #141414. Use bright, airy, soft diffused studio lighting from a slightly high angle, gently lighting the face and creating subtle catchlights in the eyes, giving a sense of expertise and care.

Shoot with an 85mm f/1.8 portrait lens look, shallow depth of field, perfect focus on the eyes, and soft natural bokeh. Show crisp white coat fabric texture, individual hair detail, and natural realistic skin texture. The mood should communicate confidence, reliability, and medical professionalism. Use clean bright cinematic color grading with subtle warmth and balanced tones.

## Negative Prompt
No cropping, no distorted facial features, no artificial smoothing, no cartoon style, no extra objects, no background clutter.
