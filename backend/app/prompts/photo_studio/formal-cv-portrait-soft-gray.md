# Formal CV Portrait Soft Gray

## Intent

Formal CV, LinkedIn, and official profile portrait.

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic formal portrait of the same man from the reference image, preserving his real identity, facial proportions, beard, hairstyle, and natural appearance. He is standing straight in a classic professional pose, facing the camera directly with symmetrical posture. Dress him in a black suit, white shirt, and black tie. Use a neutral studio background in soft gray tones. The framing should show the upper body, including shoulders and upper chest, with a balanced centered composition. Use soft professional studio lighting, realistic skin details, sharp eyes, and a clean business-style finish suitable for CV, LinkedIn, or official profile use. Expression should be calm, natural, and confident, with a very slight professional smile.

## Negative Prompt

Avoid stylization, avoid beauty filter effects, avoid unnatural symmetry, avoid over-editing, avoid warped facial features, avoid dramatic fashion lighting.

## Identity Preservation

Preserve real identity, facial proportions, beard, hairstyle, and natural appearance from the reference image.

## Wardrobe

Black suit, white shirt, and black tie.

## Lighting

Soft professional studio lighting with realistic skin details and sharp eyes.

## Background

Neutral soft gray studio background.

## Output Rules

Upper-body portrait with shoulders and upper chest visible, centered composition, calm confident expression, very slight professional smile.
