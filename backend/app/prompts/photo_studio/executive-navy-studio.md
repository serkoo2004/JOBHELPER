# Executive Navy Studio

## Intent
Authoritative executive profile photo for LinkedIn, executive resumes, or formal business presentations.

## Prompt
Create a new photorealistic high-resolution executive profile photo from the reference image. Preserve the person's exact face structure, identity, facial proportions, beard, hair, skin tone, and core features. Frame the subject from the chest upward with enough negative space above the head; do not crop the top of the head or hair. The subject looks directly into the camera with a confident, authoritative expression, with the body positioned in a subtle three-quarter angle.

Dress the subject in a premium navy suit, white dress shirt, and simple tie. Use a plain neutral studio background in #141414. Use bright, airy, soft diffused studio lighting from a slightly high angle, gently lighting the face and creating subtle catchlights in the eyes, giving a sense of leadership and authority.

Shoot with an 85mm f/1.8 portrait lens look, shallow depth of field, perfect focus on the eyes, and soft natural bokeh. Show crisp suit fabric texture, individual hair detail, and natural realistic skin texture. The mood should communicate confidence, professionalism, and executive presence. Use clean bright cinematic color grading with subtle warmth and balanced tones.

## Negative Prompt
No cropping, no distorted facial features, no artificial smoothing, no cartoon style, no extra objects, no background clutter.
