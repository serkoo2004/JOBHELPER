# Corporate Headshot Dark Gray

## Intent

High-end LinkedIn profile photo or formal business headshot.

## Prompt

Create a photorealistic professional corporate headshot of the same man from the reference image. Preserve his real facial structure, beard color and shape, hair color, skin tone, and overall likeness. He should be wearing a well-fitted black suit, white dress shirt, and black tie. Pose him in a clean studio setting with a neutral dark gray background. Use soft diffused studio lighting with gentle shadows, realistic skin texture, sharp focus on the face, and a polished but natural professional look. Frame the image as a chest-up portrait, with the subject slightly turned toward the camera, maintaining direct eye contact and a calm, confident expression. Make it look like a high-end LinkedIn profile photo or formal business headshot.

## Negative Prompt

No exaggerated facial features, no artificial skin smoothing, no cartoon effect, no distorted hands, no extra objects, no background clutter.

## Identity Preservation

Preserve the real facial structure, beard color and shape, hair color, skin tone, and overall likeness from the reference image.

## Wardrobe

Well-fitted black suit, white dress shirt, and black tie.

## Lighting

Soft diffused studio lighting with gentle shadows.

## Background

Neutral dark gray clean studio background.

## Output Rules

Chest-up portrait, slightly turned toward the camera, direct eye contact, calm and confident expression, photorealistic finish.
