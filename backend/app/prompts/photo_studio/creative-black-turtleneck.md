# Creative Professional

## Intent
Creative professional portrait for portfolios, design agencies, or artistic professional profiles.

## Prompt
Create a new photorealistic high-resolution creative professional profile photo from the reference image. Preserve the person's exact face structure, identity, facial proportions, beard, hair, skin tone, and core features. Frame the subject from the chest upward with enough negative space above the head; do not crop the top of the head or hair. The subject looks directly into the camera with a warm, creative expression, with the body slightly angled and one shoulder subtly forward.

Dress the subject in a well-fitted black turtleneck, styled for a professional studio portrait. Use a plain neutral studio background in #141414. Use bright, airy, soft diffused studio lighting from a slightly high angle, gently lighting the face and creating subtle catchlights in the eyes, giving a sense of artistic vision and innovation.

Shoot with an 85mm f/1.8 portrait lens look, shallow depth of field, perfect focus on the eyes, and soft natural bokeh. Show crisp turtleneck fabric texture, individual hair detail, and natural realistic skin texture. The mood should communicate confidence, creativity, and artistic professionalism. Use clean bright cinematic color grading with subtle warmth and balanced tones.

## Negative Prompt
No cropping, no distorted facial features, no artificial smoothing, no cartoon style, no extra objects, no background clutter.
