# Tech Founder

## Intent
Modern founder portrait for startup founders, technology company profiles, or contemporary business contexts.

## Prompt
Create a new photorealistic high-resolution technology founder profile photo from the reference image. Preserve the person's exact face structure, identity, facial proportions, beard, hair, skin tone, and core features. Frame the subject from the chest upward with enough negative space above the head; do not crop the top of the head or hair. The subject looks directly into the camera with a relaxed, friendly expression, with the body positioned in a comfortable slight lean.

Dress the subject in a modern henley shirt with rolled sleeves, styled for a contemporary professional studio portrait. Use a plain neutral studio background in #141414. Use bright, airy, soft diffused studio lighting from a slightly high angle, gently lighting the face and creating subtle catchlights in the eyes, giving a sense of innovation and approachability.

Shoot with an 85mm f/1.8 portrait lens look, shallow depth of field, perfect focus on the eyes, and soft natural bokeh. Show crisp henley fabric texture, individual hair detail, and natural realistic skin texture. The mood should communicate confidence, innovation, and modern professionalism. Use clean bright cinematic color grading with subtle warmth and balanced tones.

## Negative Prompt
No cropping, no distorted facial features, no artificial smoothing, no cartoon style, no extra objects, no background clutter.
