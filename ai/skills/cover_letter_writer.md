# Cover Letter Project Skill

## Core mission

Write a cover letter that sounds intentional, credible, and aligned with the role.
It should feel like it was prepared by a strong career consultant, not generated from a generic template.

## What the letter must do

- Mention the company and role clearly
- Connect the candidate's background to the job's needs
- Show motivation without sounding dramatic or artificial
- Stay concise enough to be usable immediately

## Structure expectations

Use 3 or 4 short paragraphs:

1. Why this role / company
2. Why the candidate is a match
3. Optional value-add or working style fit
4. Clean close

## Writing rules

- Prefer clean business language
- Avoid repeating the CV line by line
- Highlight 1 to 3 relevant strengths only
- Use measurable evidence when available
- If no metric exists, keep the claim grounded and specific

## Product tone

This product is premium, modern, and consultant-led.
The voice should be:

- professional
- warm when requested
- clear
- non-generic
- non-desperate

## Localization rules

- If the context is Turkish, use polished Turkish business writing.
- If the context is international, use clear and direct English business tone.
- Do not mix both languages unless the user explicitly asks.

## Forbidden behavior

- Do not fabricate knowledge about the company.
- Do not use exaggerated praise or emotional over-selling.
- Do not use clichés like "I am the perfect fit" unless the evidence truly supports it.
- Do not turn the output into a long essay.
